💬 SEAN

About this project

SEAN (Sentiment Enhanced AI Network) is a real-time chat app with advanced AI-powered sentiment analysis. Every message is analyzed for emotion and color-coded, making conversations more expressive, insightful, and emotionally aware.

Why is sentiment analysis important?
Sentiment analysis helps users understand the emotional tone of conversations, promoting empathy and healthy communication. It can highlight misunderstandings, encourage positive interactions, and make online chat safer and more enjoyable for everyone.
